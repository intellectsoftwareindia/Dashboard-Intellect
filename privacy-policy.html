1. Introduction

Welcome to Admin Dashboard ("App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Intellect Software Solution Pvt. Ltd.

Admin Dashboard is an administrative and analytics application that allows authorized users to securely access dashboard information, client information, trading-related analytics, performance metrics, and other administrative information provided through our backend services.

The App is intended for authorized professional and administrative users. Access to the App is controlled by the organization or administrator responsible for providing access.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information processed when you use the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect or process, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App and our backend services operate, we may collect and process the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and Login Information

The App is intended for authorized users only. Users do not create or register accounts directly through the App.

Login credentials, including a user ID and password, are provided by the organization or administrator responsible for granting access to the App.

When a user logs in, the App sends the provided authentication credentials to the authorized backend service for verification. Following successful authentication, the backend service provides an authentication token that is used to authorize subsequent requests to protected services.

The App may process:

  • User ID or username
  • Password or authentication credentials during login
  • Authentication tokens
  • Login status
  • Account or user identifiers associated with your organization

Passwords are used for authentication purposes and should not be stored by the App in plain text.

Access to the App and the information available within it may depend on the permissions and authorization assigned to the user by the organization or administrator.

2.2 Dashboard and Business Information

The App may display and process information received from our backend services, which may include:

  • Client information
  • Broker information
  • Trading-related information
  • Trading conversion statistics
  • Active client statistics
  • Connection statistics
  • Turnover information
  • Segment and channel analytics
  • Intraday trading information
  • User or administrative information
  • Other business and operational analytics

The exact information available to a user depends on the user's authorization and the backend services connected to the App.

2.3 Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store limited information locally on your device to maintain authentication and application functionality.

This may include:

  • Authentication tokens
  • Login status
  • Application preferences or configuration data

Authentication tokens may be stored locally so that the user can remain authenticated while using the App without repeatedly entering their credentials.

The App does not intentionally use local device storage to collect unrelated personal information.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

The App may receive limited technical information necessary for operation, security, troubleshooting, and compatibility, depending on the Android operating system and services used by the App.

Such information may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Technical diagnostic information
  • Network-related technical information

We do not intentionally collect precise location information through the App.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

The App is designed so that authentication credentials are used for authentication and are not intentionally stored in plain text by the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
